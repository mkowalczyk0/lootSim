# Raw generations

`ferryman-idle/` is the generation behind the shipped `boss.ferryman` strip.

`*-cast/` are **rejected** — three attempts at a cast wind-up that are kept as the evidence
behind the finding in `docs/animation.md` ("Wind-ups: what does not work"), not as art
waiting to be wired. `python3 art/anim/windup-check.py art/anim/raw/<dir>` reproduces the
measurement that rejected them. Do not strip them into the atlas.
